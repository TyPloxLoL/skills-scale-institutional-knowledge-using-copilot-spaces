# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Lead

### Role Summary
The Delivery Lead keeps the work moving across the team by coordinating execution cadence, monitoring milestone health, and aligning cross-functional work around the planned schedule.

### Responsibilities
- Drive sprint and milestone execution across teams
- Monitor progress against timeline, scope, and capacity
- Coordinate dependencies and sequencing across functions
- Support the PM in removing blockers and escalating risks
- Ensure work is ready for handoff between planning, review, and release stages

### Decision Rights
- Prioritizes delivery sequencing within agreed scope and milestones
- Can redirect team focus when risks to delivery emerge
- Escalates unresolved cross-team dependencies to the PM or sponsor as needed

### Expected Handoffs
- Works closely with Project Managers on schedule and risk tracking
- Partners with Product Managers on priority trade-offs and release readiness
- Coordinates with Developers and QA on flow, bottlenecks, and test readiness

### Typical Communication
- Delivery standups and milestone reviews
- Dependency tracking and cross-team alignment meetings
- Status updates tied to release or sprint health

---

## Product Owner / Business Sponsor

### Role Summary
The Product Owner or Business Sponsor represents the business case for the initiative. They define value, validate priority, and help resolve trade-offs between scope, cost, and timeline.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm the business outcome and expected value of the initiative
- Prioritize features and trade-offs in partnership with the Product Manager
- Approve scope changes or business decisions that affect outcomes
- Validate that delivered work meets stakeholder expectations and success metrics
- Sponsor escalation when strategic priorities or organizational constraints require action

### Decision Rights
- Final decision-maker for business priority and value trade-offs
- Approves scope and outcome changes that materially affect the project
- Resolves stakeholder conflicts when they are not otherwise addressed by delivery roles

### Expected Handoffs
- Hands off prioritized outcomes and constraints to the Product Manager and Delivery Lead
- Receives status updates and escalation summaries from the PM
- Confirms readiness for release or major milestone decisions with leadership stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder briefings and project decision forums
- Business reviews, roadmap updates, and value validation sessions
- Escalations tied to financial, customer, or strategic impact

---

## Change Coordinator

### Role Summary
The Change Coordinator manages project changes, stakeholder approvals, and communication around scope or schedule adjustments so the team can adapt without losing alignment.

### Responsibilities
- Track change requests, impacts, and required approvals
- Communicate the effect of changes on scope, timeline, cost, and quality
- Maintain a clear record of decisions and stakeholder acknowledgements
- Coordinate the rollout of approved changes into backlog or delivery planning
- Support the PM in ensuring change impacts are visible to the relevant parties

### Decision Rights
- Recommends whether a requested change should be accepted, delayed, or rejected
- Validates whether the change requires sponsor, stakeholder, or product approval
- Ensures change impacts are documented before they are implemented

### Expected Handoffs
- Receives candidate changes from Product Managers, stakeholders, or delivery teams
- Escalates approved or rejected changes to the PM, Delivery Lead, and relevant owners
- Ensures Developers and QA understand any changed scope, dependencies, or release implications

### Typical Communication
- Change request reviews and impact assessments
- Approval tracking and stakeholder notifications
- Release communication tied to business or operational impact

---

## Risk & Dependency Manager

### Role Summary
The Risk & Dependency Manager focuses on the project’s cross-team uncertainties, escalations, and mitigation paths so risk is surfaced early and handled consistently.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain the risk register and dependency log
- Assess impact, likelihood, and ownership for identified risks
- Proactively identify cross-team dependencies and blockers
- Coordinate mitigation planning and escalation paths
- Report status and trends during project reviews and planning meetings

### Decision Rights
- Recommends risk treatment, mitigation priority, or escalation level
- Flags when risk tolerance is exceeded or when a dependency requires leadership intervention
- Coordinates with PMs and sponsors on dependency decisions that affect delivery commitments

### Expected Handoffs
- Works with Project Managers on risk reporting and escalation
- Advises Delivery Lead and team leads on mitigation planning
- Shares updated risk status with Product Managers and stakeholders when business impact changes

### Typical Communication
- Weekly risk reviews and dependency tracking forums
- Escalation summaries for leadership and sponsor decision-making
- Status updates tied to milestone or release risk

---

## Role Interaction Overview

These roles are designed to reduce ambiguity while preserving clear accountability:

- The Project Manager owns coordination, schedule integrity, and communication flow.
- The Product Manager owns customer value, prioritization, and feature readiness.
- The Delivery Lead owns execution momentum, milestone health, and cross-functional sequencing.
- The Product Owner / Business Sponsor owns business decisions, value validation, and strategic alignment.
- The Change Coordinator owns the process for scope and requirement adjustments.
- The Risk & Dependency Manager owns risk visibility, dependency tracking, and escalation management.

Together, these personas strengthen decision-making across planning, execution, quality, and stakeholder communication without replacing the existing core roles. They create a clearer structure for accountability, handoffs, and escalation across the project lifecycle.
